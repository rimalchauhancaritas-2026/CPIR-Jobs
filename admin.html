AUTOMATED COLLECTION WORKSPACE

Smart Job Collector

Approved source feeds will place matching vacancies here for Caritas review. No vacancy is published automatically.

Collection rules: Local Rawalpindi/Islamabad jobs, international/overseas vacancies, and remote roles with clearly stated eligibility. Expired vacancies and duplicates are excluded or flagged. Each listing must retain its original source link. A daily scheduled collector will only be activated for sources that provide an approved API, RSS/feed, employer feed, email alert, or written permission. Sample records are clearly marked and can never be moved into live Draft jobs.

Source Registry

Manage each provider's approved connection here. Mark a source as Approved only after written permission or a clearly permitted API/feed is confirmed. Enabling a source does not start collection until a server-side connector is built and tested.
