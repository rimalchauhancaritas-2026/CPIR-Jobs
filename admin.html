Collection and verification checklist

  1. Open a source and find a current vacancy.
  2. Confirm the employer and that the original advertisement is genuine.
  3. Check that the job is in Rawalpindi/Islamabad or explicitly open to eligible applicants there.
  4. Record the exact vacancy page URL (not just the source homepage), application URL and deadline.
  5. Check qualification, experience, job type and application instructions against the original notice.
  6. Search the portal for an existing listing before adding it. Keep the source name and original URL for traceability.
  7. Save as Draft. An authorised Caritas administrator reviews the details before publishing.

Please do not use bots or automated scraping on job websites. Automatic feeds or APIs will only be added after the provider grants access or confirms permission. Do not copy listings whose reuse is prohibited.
